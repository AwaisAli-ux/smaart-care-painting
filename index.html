Questions

Parents usually ask

Is this a recurring subscription?

Yes. Every plan renews automatically at the end of its period (monthly, every 3 months, every 6 months or yearly) until you cancel. You can cancel anytime from your portal and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do kids color?

They pick a drawing in the portal, choose a color and tap any part of the picture to fill it. There are brushes for free drawing, an eraser, and undo. When they finish, they tap Submit and get a score out of 10 for how closely their colors match the example, with a little celebration. Then they tap Save, give the picture a name, and find it later (with its score) in My Artwork.

What devices does it work on?

Any tablet, phone or computer with a modern web browser. Nothing to install. Tablets give little ones the most room to color.

What ages is it for?

Kids from 3 to 12. Each drawing is marked Easy or Medium so you can pick the right one.

What is your refund policy?

You can request a full refund within 3 days of purchase or renewal. See our Refund Policy.
